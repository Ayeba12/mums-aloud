# PRODUCT.md

Product truth for mumsaloud.com. Visual decisions live in DESIGN.md; route strategy lives in surface briefs.

## What it is

Mums Aloud is a community and support organisation for Black mothers in the UK, particularly of African and Afro-Caribbean heritage. Its unique mechanism: support from mothers who have lived the same experience, so a mother is understood without having to explain.

## Who acts, and what they do

- **Primary:** a Black mother in the UK, often raising children far from the family that would have held her. Success is joining Mama's Escape (the community). She usually reads on a phone, often at night.
- **Secondary:** a mother ready for closer support (Work With Us coaching), and organisations that commission partnership, training or research.

## The offers

- **Mama's Escape:** the community. Mentor mums, expert sessions and training, resource sharing, community events. Joining is through a short form (name; what we can call you; stage: expecting, newborn, under five, other; birthday; one line on what brought you here). The Welcome Book and the full depth live inside, never on the site.
- **Podcast** (Mama's Escape on Spotify) and **newsletter:** ways to stay close without joining.
- **Work With Us:** Chaos to Calm (90 days), the Postpartum Transition Program, The Empowered Life Assessment; booked through Calendly.
- **Partnership** and **Training, Workshops & Events** for organisations.

## Proof that is real

- Founder Bokun Imoukhuede's own story.
- The MBRRACE-UK finding that Black women in the UK are around three times more likely to die in and around pregnancy.
- 70+ mothers supported through the Uplifting Mothers Project; partners including the NHS, Trafford Council and Manchester City Council (logos and figures pending owner confirmation).

## Brand commitments

- Voice: warm, direct, respectful, culturally specific, UK English. "You", never "we offer". No fear-selling and no promised outcomes.
- The covenant: what is shared inside stays inside; no mother becomes anyone's story.
- Fonts, palette, the single marigold accent, pill buttons and the People Work Home B/C composition are pinned (DESIGN.md).

## Constraints

- AAA text contrast (7:1) for routine text; 48px targets; motion off under reduced motion.
- Stock photography is a placeholder and is never captioned as members; no quotes without consent.
- No join button may lead to a closed form; no newsletter form may pretend to subscribe.
- Content comes from the client's copy and brief; claims are not invented.
